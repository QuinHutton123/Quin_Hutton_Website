Featured work

Choosing the roll centre heights, worked twice so the two answers could be held against each other. Lateral load transfer distribution is taken as Milliken and Milliken define it, through the neutral roll axis: I built that by hand as an Excel calculator, then solved the same question live in a 9 DOF Simulink model where load transfer falls out of the suspension states rather than a steady-state formula. The heights I settled on came from where those two agreed, checked against the suspension kinematics in Adams Car.
